1. Game Overview

Hex Tournament is a hybrid esports tournament system that combines real-time competitive gaming with strategic hex-board tile placement and spell card mechanics. Teams compete in external video games, and winners earn the right to place tiles on a shared hexagonal board to accumulate victory points.

Tournament Format
  • Teams: 5 teams of 2 players each (10 players total)
  • Duration: Multi-day LAN event (typically 3-4 days)
  • Board: 91-hex hexagonal grid
  • Spells: 15 unique spell cards distributed among teams

Victory Condition

How to Win

First team to reach 50 victory points wins the tournament.

Points are earned through strategic tile placement on the board. The more connected tiles a team has, the more points they score. Controlling special "heart" hexes provides passive point income each turn.

Teams & Players

  • 5 teams compete in the tournament
  • 2 players per team (10 players total)
  • Each team is assigned a distinct color for board visualization
  • Each team has a designated starting square on the board

Team names and colors are configured during tournament setup and displayed throughout the interface for easy identification.

2. The Board

The game is played on a 91-hex hexagonal board arranged in a honeycomb pattern. The board uses an axial coordinate system where each hex is identified by two coordinates: q (column) and r (row).

Hex Types

Type Count Effect
Mountain Heart 1 +2 points per turn while controlled (center of board)
Side Hearts 6 +1 point per turn while controlled (ring around center)
Starting Squares 6 Each team's first tile must be placed here
Rooms ~12 Draw 1 spell card when placing a tile here
Normal Hexes ~66 Standard placement areas, no special effect

Mountain Heart (Vuorisydän)

Located at the center of the board (coordinates q0, r0), the Mountain Heart is the most valuable position. The team controlling this hex receives +2 victory points after each match is played. Control is fiercely contested.

Side Hearts (Sivusydämet)

Six heart hexes arranged in a ring around the Mountain Heart. Each provides +1 victory point per turn to the controlling team. Strategically positioned to encourage expansion from the edges toward the center.

Rooms (Huoneet)

Special hexes scattered across the board. When a team places a tile on a room, they draw one spell card from the spell deck. Rooms incentivize strategic detours to gain card advantage.

Coordinate System

The board uses axial coordinates with bounds:

  • -5 ≤ q ≤ 5
  • -5 ≤ r ≤ 5
  • -5 ≤ q + r ≤ 5
Key Coordinates
Mountain Heart: q0r0 (center)
Starting Squares: q0r-5, q5r-5, q5r0, q0r5, q-5r5, q-5r0 (corners)

3. Tile Placement

When a team wins a match, they earn the right to place one tile on the board and handle any events from the placement.

Placement Rules

First Tile

If the team has no tiles yet, place in a free starting square.

Subsequent Tiles

  • Must be adjacent to an existing tile owned by the same team
  • Cannot be placed on hexes already occupied by another team

Placement Effects

Hex Type Effect
Room Draw 1 spell card
Unclaimed Side Heart Take control + Draw 1 spell card
Unclaimed Mountain Heart Take control
Regular Area No special effect

Turn Queue

When multiple teams win a match together (as allies in a team game), they are all added to the turn queue. Teams then place tiles one at a time in queue order:

  1. Match ends, winning team(s) identified
  2. All winners added to turn queue
  3. First team in queue places their tile
  4. Points recalculated immediately
  5. Next team places their tile
  6. Process repeats until queue is empty
  7. Return to match planning phase

4. Scoring System

Points are earned through winning matches and controlling heart hexes.

Victory Points from Winning

When your team wins a match and both team members played on the same side:

  • +1 victory point for the team
  • Personal victory points (tracked separately)
  • Right to place a tile on the board

Split teams (members on opposite sides) mark their own points individually.

Heart Control (Passive Income)

Controlling heart hexes provides passive point income after each match:

Heart Type Points Per Match
Side Heart (×6) +1 point each
Mountain Heart +2 points
Maximum Heart Income

Controlling all hearts: +8 points per match
(6 Side Hearts × 1) + (1 Mountain Heart × 2) = 6 + 2 = 8

5. Match System

External video game matches are the core competitive element. Winners earn the right to place tiles on the board.

Match Flow

  1. Planning: Admin schedules match (manual or auto-suggested)
  2. Team Formation: Players assigned to Team A vs Team B
  3. External Play: Match played in the chosen video game
  4. Result Confirmation: Admin confirms winning team(s)
  5. Tile Placement: Winners place tiles on the board
  6. Point Calculation: Scores automatically updated
  7. Win Check: System checks if any team reached 50 points
Match Types
  • 1v1: One player from each team
  • 2v2: Both players from each team
  • Mixed (5v5): Multiple teams ally together temporarily

Available Games

6. Heart Challenges

If a heart hex is already controlled by another team, you can challenge for it by placing a tile adjacent to that heart.

Challenge Trigger

Place a tile adjacent to an opponent-controlled heart hex to initiate a challenge for that heart.

Challenge Rules

  • Forced Match: Challenger and defender must play against each other in the next match
  • Game Selection: The challenger chooses which game to play
  • Stakes: Winner gains or keeps control of the heart hex

Challenge Outcomes

Result Outcome
Challenger wins Challenger takes control of the heart
Defender wins Defender keeps control of the heart
Multiple Challenges in One Turn

If multiple heart challenges occur that cannot be resolved in one game, challenge matches are played consecutively before other board changes. Heart control points are only counted once per turn, not separately for each challenge game.

7. Spell Cards

Spells are powerful effects that can be played by any team at any time.

One Spell Per Turn

Each team can only play one spell per turn.

Spell Timing Categories

Type When to Cast Example
Pre-Game Before a match starts Double-sided Glow
Instant During match or placement Mountain Purification
Tactical Strategic moment (one-time) All According to Plan
Defensive After losing a match Elf Protection
Aggressive With tile placement Calculated Aggression
Permanent Anytime (lasts all game) Victory Strike
Counter In response to opponent Rift of Deep Knowledge

All Spells

Pre-Game Spells

Double-sided Glow / Aura of Supremacy Pre-Game

If your team finishes in 1st or 2nd place in the next match, you receive double points and double tiles for that victory.

Cast: Before match starts

Get Away From Me! Pre-Game

If your team wins the next match, destroy all opponent tiles adjacent to your tiles on the board.

Cast: Before match starts

Domination! Domination! Domination! Pre-Game

If your team wins 3 games in a row, gain +2 bonus tiles and +2 bonus points after the third win.

Cast: Before starting a win streak attempt

Sabotage Pre-Game

Ban one game element (hero, weapon, strategy, etc.) from an opponent's loadout for the next game.

Cast: Before match starts

Defensive Spells

Elf Protection Defensive

After losing a match, protect all your tiles from destruction. Also grants immunity to negative spell effects for the next game.

Cast: Immediately after losing a match

Tactical Spells

All According to My Plan Tactical (Rare)

Copy any spell an opponent just used. The copied spell takes effect immediately with the same targets/effects.

Cast: Immediately after opponent uses a spell

Complete the Contract Tactical

Challenge for control of the Mountain Heart, regardless of position. If you win the challenge, gain +2 bonus points in addition to control.

Cast: During your turn, triggers immediate challenge

Instant Spells

Mountain Purification Instant

Immediately gain +1 point for each heart hex your team currently controls.

Cast: After placing a tile (best when controlling multiple hearts)

Aggressive Spells

Calculated Aggression Aggressive

Place 1 tile. If that tile touches any enemy tiles, destroy all adjacent enemy tiles.

Cast: When placing a tile adjacent to enemies

Placement Spells

Knowledge from the Deep Placement

Place 2 tiles anywhere on the board. Restriction: cannot place adjacent to heart hexes or enemy tiles.

Cast: During placement phase

Event Spells

Double Bid Event Modifier

When you would draw an event card (from placing on a Room), draw 2 cards instead of 1. One-time use.

Cast: When placing tile on a Room hex

Permanent Spells

Victory Strike Permanent Buff

For the rest of the tournament, each time your team captures a new heart hex, gain +1 bonus point immediately.

Cast: Anytime (best early for maximum value)

Debuff Spells

Echo of Silence Debuff

Choose one opponent player. That player cannot speak or communicate verbally during the next match.

Cast: Before match starts

Betting from the Depths Bet/Gamble

Make 3 predictions about the upcoming match. For each correct prediction, gain +1 point/tile. If all 3 are wrong, lose 2 tiles.

Cast: Before match starts

Counter Spells

Rift of Deep Knowledge Counter (Rare)

Keep this card in hand. When an opponent would draw an event card, counter that draw - they receive nothing.

Cast: In response to opponent's Room hex placement

8. Special Rules

Victory

The tournament ends when a team reaches 50 points.

Point Calculation

Heart income points are awarded after:

  • Each match result is confirmed
  • Based on which hearts each team controls at that moment

Heart control can change due to:

  • Tile placement on a heart hex
  • Challenge results (winning a challenge for an opponent's heart tile)
  • Spell effects

Undo/Redo

Admins have access to an undo/redo system for correcting mistakes. All actions are logged with timestamps for accountability. This should only be used for genuine errors, not strategic "takebacks."

Disconnections & Pauses

If a player disconnects during an external match, the standard rules of that game apply. Tournament state is persisted in Firebase and survives page refreshes. Admins can pause tournament progression if needed.
