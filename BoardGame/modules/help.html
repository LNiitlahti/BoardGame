💬 Frequently Asked Questions

🎮 Tournaments

How do I create a new tournament?
Click the tournament selector dropdown in the navbar and select "➕ Create New Tournament". You'll need admin or god privileges to create tournaments.
How do I switch between tournaments?
Use the tournament selector dropdown in the navbar to select a different tournament. Your view will update to show data for that tournament.

👥 Teams

How do I assign players to teams?
Navigate to the Teams section from the sidebar. Select a team and use the "Assign Players" button to add players to that team.
Can I view my team's dashboard?
Yes! If you're assigned to a team, click "My Team" in the sidebar to view your team dashboard with matches, stats, and more.

⚔️ Matches

How do I create a match?
Go to the Matches section from the sidebar and click "Create Match". Select the teams, set the match details, and save.
How do I record match results?
Find the match in the Matches section, click on it to open details, and use the "Record Result" button to enter the winner and final scores.

🎭 User Roles

🔱 GOD
Complete system control with access to all features, tournaments, and administrative functions.
👑 ADMIN
Manage tournaments, teams, matches, and players. Can switch to player view to see team dashboards.
🎮 PLAYER
View team dashboard, check match schedules, and participate in tournaments.
